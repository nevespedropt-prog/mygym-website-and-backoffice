# MY GYM London back office (admin site)

Static site (no build step). Real data from Supabase through the `admin_*` database functions (see `app/supabase/migration-admin-backend.sql`). Roles: owner/admin, manager, reception, coach.

The member app has no admin pages and never will: the app holds only the timetable, bookings, workout logs and body measurements. Everything for staff lives here, in a web browser.

## Before it can work (one time)
1. Apply `app/supabase/migration-admin-backend.sql` and `migration-admin-backend-4.sql` (timetable editor) to the live database (adds the `staff` table and the functions). Both are applied live. A rolled-back test run on 2026-10-28 passed: owner sees everything; coach sees only dashboard, schedule and health forms; reception sees members and enquiries but not health forms; signed-out and non-staff people are refused.
2. The owner is admin because `profiles.is_admin` is already true for the owner's app account. Add staff later in the back office under Team and settings (they need an app account first).

## Hosting: Cloudflare Pages (admin.mygymlondon.co.uk)
1. Cloudflare dashboard, Workers & Pages, Create, Pages, Connect to Git: repo `mygym-website-and-backoffice`, project name `mygym-admin`, production branch `main`, root directory `admin-site`, no build command, output directory `.`. Every push to `main` then redeploys it.
2. Project, Custom domains, add `admin.mygymlondon.co.uk`. Cloudflare shows the CNAME target (`mygym-admin.pages.dev`).
3. DNS is held at Wix: add a `CNAME` record, host `admin`, pointing to that target. Wait for it to resolve.
4. Open https://admin.mygymlondon.co.uk and sign in with the owner's app email and password.
5. Check: Dashboard loads, Members shows the real members, Schedule shows today's classes. Turn on 2FA for the owner's accounts.
Security headers come from `_headers` (Cloudflare). `.htaccess` is only for Apache hosts and is ignored on Cloudflare.

## What is not here yet

- Test with fake data locally: serve this folder with any static server and replace `vendor/supabase.js` with a stub; do not point it at real data from a shared computer.

## Timetable and launch day
The Timetable page adds, edits, switches off and removes classes and gym hours. Until launch day the website (Wix) still drives the timetable through the `sync-timetable` function, so Wix-sourced rows are put back every 10 minutes. On launch day the owner (admin only) presses "Take control of the timetable": this turns the website sync off (`app_flags` key `timetable_backoffice` = on) and turns every active Wix row into a normal row. To undo: set that flag's mode to `off`; check for doubled classes afterwards.

## Campaigns (Marketing page, admin only)
Database: `migration-marketing-campaigns.sql` (applied). Edge functions: `admin-campaign`, `send-scheduled-campaigns`, `resend-webhook`.
- **History:** every campaign is a row in `marketing_campaigns` (draft, scheduled, sending, sent). The page lists them with status and results; click one to open it. A sent campaign is read-only and can be duplicated as a new draft; if some emails failed it offers "Retry". Sent campaigns cannot be deleted from the page.
- **Audience (segments):** everyone who confirmed | members (have an app account) | website sign-ups without an app account | active members (booked in the last 30 days). Only people with confirmed, not withdrawn consent are ever included; `send-email` checks consent again at send time.
- **Service notices (one-off, no marketing consent):** "New email" asks what kind: *News or offer* (marketing, subscribers only) or *Service notice* (closure, timetable change, account news). A notice (`kind = 'notice'` in `marketing_campaigns`, migration `migration-notice-campaigns.sql`, applied 2026-11-13) goes to: active clients, not-active clients (paused, cancelled or ended), paused only, former only, everyone with an app account, and/or up to 300 typed email addresses. No consent is needed and there is no unsubscribe link, so the composer shows a warning, a tick-box ("this is service information, not an offer or promotion") and three starter templates (closure, timetable change, opening hours). People who ever marked an email as spam are always left out. Wix DRAFT orders are unfinished sign-ups and are not counted as clients. The exact recipient count comes from the database (`admin_notice_count`), so overlapping groups are not double counted. Delivery goes through the `notice` email in `send-email` (newsletter layout, service footer). Sent notices appear in the same history with a "Service notice" label.
- **Writing:** templates, live preview, character counts and a checklist. The red button works only when the checklist is complete, including a test email sent for the exact wording (a passed test is remembered in the browser for that draft until the wording changes). Pressing it while locked says what is missing. Sending needs a second press to confirm.
- **Send now or schedule:** "Send now" sends in batches of 40 and the page keeps going until nobody is left. "Schedule" takes a London time (2 minutes to 90 days ahead); a scheduled campaign is locked until you unschedule it. The database schedule `send-scheduled-campaigns` runs every minute, starts due campaigns and carries on any that stalled. Each person gets a campaign once (dedupe key `campaign:<id>:<email>`), so retries and overlaps never double up.
- **Delivery results:** Resend reports delivered, bounced, spam-complaint and click events to the `resend-webhook` function (verified with a Svix signature, secret `RESEND_WEBHOOK_SECRET`), which fills `email_events`. A spam complaint or a permanent bounce also withdraws that person's marketing consent. Opens are deliberately not counted (mail apps hide them).
- **Resend setup (done 2026-11-12):** webhook with events delivered, bounced, complained, clicked pointing at `https://<project>.supabase.co/functions/v1/resend-webhook`; click tracking on through the tracking subdomain `links.mygymlondon.co.uk` (CNAME to `links2.resend-dns.com`, kept at Wix like the other DNS). If DNS ever moves, copy that record too. Emails sent before tracking was switched on have no click data.
- **Origins:** `admin-campaign` and `admin-class-cancelled` only accept calls from https://admin.mygymlondon.co.uk (not the pages.dev address). From any other address a send fails with "Could not reach the server".
- **To undo the campaign system:** `select cron.unschedule('send-scheduled-campaigns');` then drop the functions and table listed at the top of the migration file.

## Class-cancelled emails (Schedule page, admin only)
"Class cancelled" checks who is booked on a date and emails them (`admin-class-cancelled` function). Before takeover, cancel the session in Wix afterwards; after takeover bookings are not removed, so switch the class off on the Timetable page if needed. Not yet tested with a real send.

## Enquiries
Website forms (free class, gym trial, contact) post to the `submit-form` function; staff are emailed a link to the Messages page here (`https://admin.mygymlondon.co.uk/#/enquiries`). The app has no forms.

## If the website host or DNS moves
This site stays on Cloudflare Pages. Only the DNS record matters: if the domain's DNS leaves Wix, copy the `admin` CNAME (to `mygym-admin.pages.dev`) to the new DNS.

## Members, templates, forms and pages (added 2026-11-12)
- **Possible duplicates (Members page header, `admin_possible_duplicates`, migration `migration-possible-duplicates.sql`):** read-only list of groups where the same phone number (last 10 digits) or the same full name appears under different email addresses, with each person's plan status. It never changes anything: merge two contacts in Wix (Contacts, select both, Merge); the list refreshes after the next 30-minute sync.
- **Editing a member (added 2026-11-13, function `admin-edit-member`, migration `migration-member-edit.sql`):** open a member. *Edit details* (owner or manager) changes first name, last name, phone and date of birth in the Wix contact, the back office copy, the app profile and the in-person record (email cannot be changed here; Wix keeps it fixed for site members). Memberships (owner only), per membership: *Pause*, *Resume*, *Stop at end of period*, *Cancel now*, *Change plan* (new plan is recorded first, then the old one is stopped; pay in person = recorded in Wix as paid, pay by card = the old plan stops and the person finishes the Wix checkout), and *Start a plan* for someone without one. Each change is checked against Wix first (only an active plan can be paused, and so on), read back from Wix afterwards, and written to the staff access log. Nothing is emailed to the member from here, and no refund or credit is calculated: refunds stay in Wix. *Check editing access* (Members page header) asks Wix, with harmless test calls on ids that do not exist, whether the API key may edit contacts, change orders and record offline orders; a cross means that permission must be added to the key in Wix. The Wix calls were tested against a mock of Wix, not against real clients.
- **Members:** your real client list. Wix contacts, plans and bookings are copied every 30 minutes by `sync-wix-clients` (read-only on the Wix side). Tabs: Active, Not training (no booking for 21 days), Payment failed, Paused, Former, Leads, Everyone; filter by membership type. Click a name for the full profile (plan history, payments, app account, bookings, forms, emails, consent, private notes). Export and import CSV lists; "Bring in Wix subscribers" on the Marketing page copies the people subscribed in Wix (those who unsubscribed in Wix are blocked).
- **Add member (in person):** details, plan, health form, agreements, optional app login, all recorded as "taken in person by <staff> on <date>". Done by the `admin-add-member` function: creates the Wix contact and site member, records the plan as paid in person (Wix offline order) or opens the Wix checkout for card (card numbers are never typed here), optionally invites them to the app. Clients with no email are kept in the back office only. Needs the Wix API key to be allowed to create members/contacts and register offline orders; if Wix refuses, the form says which step failed and the rest is still saved.
- **Plans and payments / dashboard:** real figures from the Wix orders copy (monthly income, active members by plan, failed payments, paused, joined/left in 30 days).
- **Email templates:** your own library (create, edit, duplicate, delete). Every new campaign starts from a template, and "Send this" on a template opens the campaign page with it filled in. All emails share the same MY GYM look. The automatic emails live in code (`app/supabase/email-templates`), but the **Emails page now edits their wording**: open a group, press *Edit wording* (subject, preview line, heading, button text and every paragraph; placeholders such as `{{first_name}}` are checked against what that email can fill, and a wrong one is refused), *Send me a test* (to your own address, example names, subject starts with [TEST]), *Back to the original*, or *Switch off* (two presses; nobody gets that email until you switch it on). Changes are rows in `email_overrides` (migration `migration-email-wording-editor.sql`, applied 2026-11-13) read by `send-email` v15 at send time, so they apply to the very next email; the built-in wording is restored the moment the row is removed. The list and each email's defaults come from `admin-site/auto-emails.json` (generated from the live email library; regenerate it with `scratch gen_auto_emails.mjs` logic whenever an email in `templates.mjs` changes, otherwise the editor shows stale defaults). Not editable: the sign-in emails (sent by Supabase Auth), the campaign layout, the service notice, the details table, highlighted box, bullet list and small print inside each email (shown read-only in the editor).
- **Audiences:** everyone on the list, active clients, Gym + Classes, Classes only, not training, paused, former, app members, website sign-ups. Only people who agreed to news and offers are ever included.
- **Forms and landing pages you build yourself** (Forms and pages): questions, notifications and the confirmation email use the standard templates; answers arrive in Messages. Each form and page gets a link on `go.html` (set `GO_BASE` in `config.js`; after the website move change it to https://www.mygymlondon.co.uk/go.html and make sure go.html is on that site). Served by the public `forms-public` function (spam trap, minimum fill time, 8 per hour per connection).
- **Birthdays:** date of birth is asked in the app profile; a birthday email goes to subscribed members at 09:15.
- **Coaches:** the class editor suggests coaches and accepts new names; "Coaches by day" sets who teaches each day (default Pedro Sunday to Friday, James Saturday).
- Edge function sources for `admin-add-member` and `forms-public` can be read back from Supabase (Edge Functions) if the repo copy is missing.
