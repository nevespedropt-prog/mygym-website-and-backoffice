FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about memberships, booking, opening hours and personal training.

Do I need a contract?

No. There is no contract and no sneaky joining fees. Classes start from £6.

What are your opening hours?

We are open every day from 5am until midnight.

Where are you?

Main Avenue, Bush Hill Park, Enfield, EN1 1DS.

How do I book a class or a gym slot?

Members book, cancel and reschedule in the MyGym app (app.mygymlondon.co.uk) or online in two taps. Book MyGym gym slots are available to Gym & Exercise Class members.

Can I just pay as I go?

Yes. PAYG Gym is £4 per visit, with no membership and no commitment. Classes can be booked from £6 a session.

Which membership is right for me?

Gym only is £26 a month, classes only is £26 a month, and Gym & Exercise Classes is £35 a month or £400 a year. See the Services page for every option.

Do I get an induction?

Yes. The Gym & Exercise Class Membership includes a free induction and an introductory health check.

How much is personal training?

30-minute sessions start from as low as £32.50, and your first PT session is free. Small group personal training is £60 a month for 2 to 4 friends (£15 each per session, minimum 4 sessions a month).

How do I cancel my membership?

Monthly plans have no minimum term. Give us at least 1 week's notice before your next billing date. Annual plans can be refunded within 1 month of paying. After that, any refund is worked out by taking off the months you have used, and there is no refund if no full month is left. Full details are in our terms and conditions.

Can I cancel a class or gym booking?

Yes. You can cancel in the app or on our website up to 10 minutes before the session starts. Please cancel as early as you can so someone else can take your place. We do not give refunds for classes or gym sessions, but a cancelled pay as you go class becomes a credit for another class.

What is the personal training cancellation policy?

You can reschedule or cancel for free with at least 24 hours' notice. With less notice, 50% of the session price is charged and the other 50% is added as credit towards your next purchase. We do not give cash refunds for personal training. Your free first session can be used once per person.

How does the refer a friend offer work?

Get a 50% discount on your first billing cycle for each friend you refer who buys a plan. Log in to refer.

How do I get the app?

Download it today: scan the QR code on the home page, or open app.mygymlondon.co.uk on your phone. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, tap the menu, then Install app. It installs like any other app and there is nothing to look for in an app store.

Still have a question?

Email info@mygymlondon.co.uk or ask us at the gym.

Still have a question?

Send us a message and we will reply within one working day.
