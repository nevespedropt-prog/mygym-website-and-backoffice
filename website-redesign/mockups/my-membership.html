How it works

  • What Wix does today (checked): a member can see and pay a plan in the Wix member area, but none of the public memberships lets the member cancel (the "buyer can cancel" setting is off on every one), and Wix has no member self-pause. So today a member has to message the gym. Our page is easier: they see everything and send one clear request.
  • Request, not instant: pausing or cancelling changes money, so it goes to you as a request. You press one button in the back office (Pause, Cancel at end of period, Change plan), the member gets a confirmation email and the status turns to Confirmed. Nothing is cancelled by accident and nobody has to write an email.
  • Same on the website for people who do not use the app: log in, My account, same three screens. Pay as you go customers see their bookings, class credits and receipts instead of a plan.
  • Shown to the member: plan, price, start date, next payment and card, what the plan includes and what they can book, class credits, recent payments with receipt link, health form status, the notice rule from your terms, contact details. Updating the card opens the secure Wix page (we never handle card details).
  • Notice rule: from your terms (1 week before the next billing date). The form shows the earliest effective date and warns when a request comes late, so there are no surprises.
  • Payment failed or suspended: a red banner explains what happened, the retry date (5 days later) and the "Update my card" button, as in your terms.
  • For you: a "Membership requests" list in the back office with the member, what they asked, from when and why; one-tap actions; an email to you when a request arrives; everything logged.
  • Not included on purpose: refunds (done in Wix by you, as your terms say) and changing the card number here.
